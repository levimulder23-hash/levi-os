# FIE Share Code Email Template

**When to use:** As soon as you receive UKVI approval and generate your share code.

**Steps first:**
1. Open UKVI approval email → follow link to create your UKVI account
2. Log in to your UKVI account → find "Share your immigration status" → generate a share code (it's a 9-character alphanumeric code, e.g. W4X2YZ9AB)
3. Send the email below to Jake White at FIE

---

**To:** immigration@fie.org.uk  
**Subject:** Share Code — Levi Mulder, Fall 2026

Hey Jake,

I just received my UKVI approval and wanted to get my share code over to you right away.

My share code is: **[PASTE SHARE CODE HERE]**

My date of birth is: [YOUR DOB]

Please let me know if you need anything else from me to confirm my immigration status for the Fall 2026 program.

Thanks so much,  
Levi Mulder

---

**Notes:**
- Share codes expire after 90 days — send as soon as you generate it
- Your DOB is needed alongside the share code for FIE to verify
- Jake White is the FIE Immigration Officer
- FIE email might be: immigration@fie.org.uk or jake.white@fie.org.uk (confirm from your FIE enrollment emails)
