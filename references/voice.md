Samples pulled verbatim from Gmail Sent on 2026-09-10 (Levi: "look for shorter responses, those are me"). Match this register when drafting as Levi: short, direct, polite, no corporate padding, first-name sign-off. Do not fake his voice on external content without showing him a draft first.

## Register notes
- Opens with "Hey <name>," or "Goodmorning," — casual, warm.
- One idea per sentence; says what happened, then asks for next steps ("Let me know what the next steps are").
- Apologizes plainly when he owes one; no over-explaining.
- Signs off with just "Levi" or nothing.
- Never corporate ("per my last email", "circling back"). Never em dashes.
